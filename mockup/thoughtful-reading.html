Thoughtful Reading · clickable mockup

The whole plugin on a Kobo-size screen (107 × 142 mm). Tap the right side of the page to turn pages, the left side to go back, and the top edge to open the menu. Dotted lines on a page can be tapped to highlight them. Screens and menu entries marked NEW IN 0.2.0 (orange) are planned for the next version: All books, Export all books, Edit questions and questions about your lexicon cards.
